## If Lara Can't Host

### Hostels (Cheapest)

| Hostel | Price/night | Location | Vibe | Walking to hotel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Selina Nueva Córdoba** | $10-12/bed | Nueva Córdoba | Social, bar, backpacker | 15 min |
| **Faz Hostel** | $8-10/bed | Centro | Basic, cheap | 10 min |

Hostel cons: Shared room, less privacy, can be loud
