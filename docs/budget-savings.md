## Money-Saving Strategies

### 1. Bring food from Paraguay (biggest savings)
- Noodles, rice, pasta, instant soup — $10 total
- Chipa, sopa paraguaya, chipa guazú — share at room parties
- Tereré, yerba, cocido quemado — free daily drink
- Dulce de leche, alfajores — cheap snacks
- Mate cocido bags — quick caffeine

### 2. Stay with Lara (biggest single saving)
- She offered to host ("echar casa")
- Even a floor/couch saves $200+ over 2 weeks
- Confirm with her in August when she knows her housing

### 3. Bus instead of plane (if budget is tight)
- Saves ~$60 total vs flying
- Night bus saves 1 night accommodation
- But costs 30+ hours of your life — consider if that's worth $60

### 4. Hostel instead of Airbnb
- Dorm bed: $8-12/night vs Airbnb $15-25/night
- Kitchen access = cook your own food
- Social = meet fellow travelers

### 5. Free activities
- Paseo del Buen Pastor (free)
- Parque Sarmiento (free)
- Jesuit Block churches (free)
- Güemes street market (free to browse)
- Hiking in Sierras de Córdoba (free)
- Nuevocentro Shopping (free to walk, attached to hotel)

### 6. Con-specific savings
- Bring your own water bottle (fill at hotel)
- Eat before going to the con (hostel breakfast, packed lunch)
- Don't buy at Dealers Den unless you budgeted for it
- Volunteer at the con (might get perks)

### 7. Money exchange tips
- Bring USD cash (crisp bills, no tears)
- Exchange at **blue dollar cuevas** in Córdoba for ~40% better rate than official
- Use **Prex** or **Wise** card for ATM withdrawals
- DO NOT exchange at airports (worst rates)
- DO NOT use official exchange houses
- Have some ARS cash for small purchases
