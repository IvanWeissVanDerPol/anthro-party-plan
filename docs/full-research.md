# Full Research — Anthro Party Argentina 2026

## The Event

APA! is a **furry convention** (furcon) — a gathering for the furry fandom (people interested in anthropomorphic animal characters). It's the **first hotel-based furcon in Córdoba**, born from the **Furcamp Cordobés (FCC)** legacy (annual camping furmeets 2018-2025).

- **Theme**: University/graduation — "tu último año" with a horror-comedy twist
- **Ages**: +18 only (strict, enforced at door with ID)
- **Capacity**: First edition, expect 100-300 attendees
- **Language**: Spanish-first, English welcome

## The City: Córdoba, Argentina

- **Population**: ~1.5M (3rd largest in Argentina)
- **Culture**: Colonial architecture, UNESCO Jesuit Block, heavy student population
- **Spring weather (Oct)**: Avg 68°F / 20°C, pleasant, light jacket evenings
- **Walk Score from hotel**: 60/100 — somewhat walkable
- **Nearby**: Iglesia de la Compañía de Jesús (3 min), Nuevocentro Shopping (attached), Paseo del Buen Pastor, Patio Olmos, Sarmiento Park
