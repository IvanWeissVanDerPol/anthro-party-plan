# Packing List — What to Bring

| Rice | 1 kg | Base for cheap meals |
| Pasta | 1 kg | Base for cheap meals |

## Buy in Córdoba (If Needed)

- Water (bottled)
- Bread, cheese, ham for sandwiches
- Fruit (bananas, apples — cheap)
- Yerba (if you run out)
- Soap/shampoo (if you don't want to bring)
- Fernet + Coke (Córdoba tradition)

## What NOT to Bring

- Glitter, confetti, serpentines (banned by APA rules)
- Expensive jewelry or electronics
- Anything you can't afford to lose
- Too many clothes (you won't wear them all)
- ARS cash (exchange in Córdoba at blue rate)
