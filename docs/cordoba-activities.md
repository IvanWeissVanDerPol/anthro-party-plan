# Córdoba Activities — Pre & Post Con

## Free ($0)

| Activity | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Paseo del Buen Pastor** | Nueva Córdoba | Water show, art exhibits, free |
| **Parque Sarmiento** | 15 min walk from hotel | Huge park, lake, running trails |
| **Jesuit Block (UNESCO)** | Centro | Historic churches, free entry |
| **Museo Emilio Caraffa** | Near Parque Sarmiento | Free on Wednesdays |
| **Güemes Street Fair** | Güemes neighborhood | Weekends, artisan stalls |
| **Plaza San Martín** | Centro | Main square, people watch |
| **Nuevocentro Shopping** | Attached to hotel | AC, free to walk, window shop |
| **Nueva Córdoba architecture walk** | Nueva Córdoba streets | Beautiful old buildings, mansions |
| **Cañón del Río Tanti** | 1h bus from Córdoba | Free canyon, swim, hike |
| **Reserva Natural San Martín** | Near Córdoba | Free nature reserve |
| **Iglesia de los Capuchinos** | Centro | Unique architecture, free |
| **Plaza España** | Nueva Córdoba | Meeting point, fountain |
| **Teatro del Libertador** | Centro | Free to see the building |
