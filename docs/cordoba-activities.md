## Cannabis Clubs

Argentina has cannabis social clubs. In Córdoba:

| Club | What | How to join |
|---|---|---|
| **Anandamida Club** | Cannabis social club, info/culture | anandamida.store — free membership signup |
| **Frecuencia Smart Culture Place** | CBD store + smart shop | Show up |
| **Dr. Cogollo Grow Shop** | Grow shop | Buy supplies, ask about the scene |
| **El Capitan (Fast Track)** | Connects you to clubs | elcapitanbrand.com, €22 fast-track |

**Important**: Argentine cannabis clubs are private. You need membership (usually free or cheap). Bring your passport. Cannabis is NOT legal to buy on the street — only through clubs or REPROCANN (medical registry).

**Weed tip**: Bring your own from Paraguay across the border. It's illegal but the bus border check is usually quick and they don't search bags thoroughly. Or join a club once in Córdoba. Your call, be smart.

## Psychedelics

Shrooms (hongos/psilocybe) are accessible in Córdoba through:
- **Grow shops** — ask about spore prints or kits (for "research")
- **The river scene** — Tanti, Cuesta Blanca, Mina Clavero — hippie/alternative crowds, ask around
- **REPROCANN** — technically medical, some doctors prescribe more than just cannabis
- **Telegram groups** — search closer to date

October is shroom season in the Sierras de Córdoba (humid, just after spring rains). If you go to Tanti or Cuesta Blanca, keep an eye out. Ask locals.

## Important Notes

- **Weed in Argentina**: Decriminalized for personal consumption. Growing is legal with REPROCANN. Buying/selling is not. Private clubs are gray area.
- **Shrooms**: Not explicitly legal but not enforced for personal use. Collecting in nature is common.
- **LSD**: Illegal. Be discreet.
- **BDSM clubs**: Córdoba has a scene but it's underground. Best way in: meet people at the con, they'll know.
- **Walking around high**: Fine. Cops in Córdoba generally don't care about small amounts.
- **Don't smoke inside bars** — go outside. Argentine bars in Córdoba are generally smoking-allowed inside though (or at least tolerated).
