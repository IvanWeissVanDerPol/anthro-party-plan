> "Holaa! Somos Ivan y Ale, venimos de Paraguay para nuestra primera APA! Alguien tiene room party? Podemos llevar chipa y tereré para compartir <3"

**Furries love new people, especially international ones. You'll get invites.**

## How to Join Room Parties Without a Room

1. **Post in Telegram** — the main way people share party locations
2. **Hang in common areas** — hotel lobby, hallways by the con suite, near Dealers Den
3. **Meet people at panels** — sit next to someone, chat, exchange Telegram handles
4. **Lara introduces you** — she's the golden ticket
5. **Bring food from Paraguay** — chipa is the universal furry social lubricant
6. **Follow the flyers** — people leave room party flyers on bulletin boards at the con

### Room party etiquette

- Knock before entering
- Ask before touching anything
- Offer to share food/drink
- Don't overstay your welcome
- Help clean up
- If it's an AD (adults-only) room, respect the rules

## Making Friends

### Icebreakers that work
- "First time at APA? Us too!"
- "We're from Paraguay, brought chipa — want to try?"
- "Your fursuit/art/badge is awesome, who made it?"
- "Want to get tereré/mate?"

### What to offer
- **Chipa, sopa paraguaya** — everyone wants to try
- **Tereré** — share your guampa, instant bonding
- **Help** — someone needs a hand carrying stuff? help them
- **Compliments** — furry artists put their hearts into their work

### What not to do
- Don't touch someone's fursuit without asking (it's a rule — Art. 19)
- Don't assume everyone wants to cuddle/hookup
- Don't be pushy about anything
- Don't ignore "no"

## After the Con — Staying Connected

- Exchange Telegram handles with people you meet
- Ask about local furmeets in your area (Paraguay has some too!)
- Join general LATAM furry groups
